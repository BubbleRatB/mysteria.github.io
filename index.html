    • Overview

    Mysteria is a realm, a kingdom of its own kind. It harbours magic, all magic. In fact, every piece of magic to ever exists comes from this realm. Various species make up the population of Mysteria, each originating from different lands. Said lands are the four primary biomes which Mysteria is divided by: Astral Plains, Hollow Grove, Mush Lands, and Crystal Peaks. These lands have their own magical properties and species that define them from each other. Follow the link above to discover more.

    History

    Mysteria lives within a cycle of life as one of twelve realms. Each realm is inspired by one of the the Twelve Links of Independence from the Buddhist religion.

    Inspiration
